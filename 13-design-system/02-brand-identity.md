# VDS Brand Identity

Defines the Vestara logo, wordmark, icon mark, and their usage rules — spacing minimums, clear space, dark/light variants, and prohibited treatment.

## Terminal interpretation

Terminal surfaces use the plain-text wordmark `Vestara`. ASCII art is optional
and MUST NOT consume space needed for work. Runtime status appears adjacent to,
but is never merged into, the product identity. Branding MUST remain legible
when color and Unicode are unavailable.
