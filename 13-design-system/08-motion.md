# VDS Motion

Timing curves (easing profiles), duration scale, and choreography rules for transitions, micro-interactions, and page-level motion. Reduced-motion equivalent for every animation.

Terminal surfaces SHOULD communicate progress through meaningful status changes
and streamed content, not decorative spinners. Rendering MUST avoid flicker and
unnecessary full-screen redraw. Reduced-motion mode disables animated indicators
without removing liveness or cancellation feedback.
